### CollaborateLeft: A Progressive Intellectual Model

**CollaborateLeft**, as envisioned in the context of Web4, represents a transformative intellectual property model that bridges the gap between the restrictive frameworks of **Copyright** (the "old world") and the more open, community-driven ethos of **Copyleft** or donation-based systems (the "new Oasis"). It is a dynamic, flexible approach that allows creators to retain control over their work while progressively opening it up to the public, based on their values, ethics, and mission.

---

### **1. Copyright (Old World): The Starting Point**
In the traditional system, copyright grants creators exclusive rights to their intellectual property, often with the goal of monetization or control. While this model protects creators, it also creates "information fiefdoms," where access to knowledge and creativity is restricted by paywalls, licenses, or legal barriers.

CollaborateLeft acknowledges the necessity of copyright in the current world but seeks to **redefine its purpose**. Instead of using copyright as a tool for hoarding, it becomes a **starting point** for a more inclusive and mission-driven dissemination of knowledge.

---

### **2. The Transition: Tiered Access and Ethics**
CollaborateLeft introduces a **tiered system of access**, where the creator (IP holder) defines the terms under which their work is shared. This system is fluid and adaptable, allowing creators to balance their need for livelihood with their desire to contribute to the greater good.

- **Tiered Access:** The creator can set different levels of access based on trust, ethics, or contributions. For example:
  - Free access for those who volunteer time to a related cause.
  - Discounted access for those who participate in the community or mission.
  - Paid access for those who prefer a conventional transaction.

- **Ethics-Driven Sharing:** The creator declares a **code of ethics** that governs how their work can be used. This ensures that the work aligns with the creator's values, even as it becomes more widely available.

---

### **3. Copyleft and Donation-Based Models (The New Oasis)**
As the creator's mission gains traction and their livelihood becomes more secure, they can progressively transition their work into the **Copyleft** or donation-based realm. In this phase, the work becomes part of the "new Oasis," where it is freely available to the public but still tied to the creator's mission.

- **Copyleft Within Web4:** The work is made open-domain within the Web4 ecosystem, where it can be built upon, shared, and used collaboratively, as long as it adheres to the creator's declared ethics.
- **Donation-Based Support:** Instead of relying on sales, the creator receives support through **direct donations** or contributions to their mission. This model encourages a gift economy, where the value of the work is recognized and rewarded by the community it serves.

---

### **4. A Pragmatic Bridge**
CollaborateLeft is not about abandoning the old system overnight. It is a **pragmatic bridge** that allows creators to operate within the existing legal framework while gradually transitioning to a freer, more collaborative model. This ensures that creators can sustain themselves financially while contributing to the greater good.

- **Outside Web4:** The work remains under copyright, protecting the creator's rights in the traditional system.
- **Within Web4:** The work operates under CollaborateLeft, where access is governed by the creator's ethics and mission.

---

### **5. The Vision: From Fiefdoms to Freedom**
CollaborateLeft is the intellectual property model for a world where knowledge is no longer hoarded but shared. It empowers creators to:
- Retain control over their work.
- Define the terms of its use.
- Transition to a system where their work serves a mission, not just a market.

This progressive model ensures that creators can thrive while contributing to a nurturing, collaborative digital ecosystem. It is the cornerstone of Web4's vision for a kinder, more purposeful internet.
