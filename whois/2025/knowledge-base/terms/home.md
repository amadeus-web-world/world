## Proprietary Process / Code

### Salient Features of CollaborateLeft (Points for Agreement)

**1. Declaration of Ethics**  

- The IP holder defines a clear code of ethics to govern the use of their work, ensuring alignment with their values.

**2. Tiered Access System**  
Access to the IP is divided into levels. Which level do you think is applicable

* **Basic Access:** Free or low-cost for general use.
* **Intermediate Access:** Paid access for commercial or specialized use.
* **Collaborative Access:** Reserved for deep partnerships and co-creation.

**3. Transparency in Terms**  

* The IP holder specifies initial terms, including usage rights, consulting fees, and ethical boundaries, which can evolve over time.

**4.Iterative Collaboration**  

* Both parties engage in regular feedback loops to refine the collaboration and ensure alignment with shared goals.

**5.Mutual Growth**  

* The relationship deepens over time, with both parties clarifying intentions for future engagements.

**6. Flexibility in Engagement**  

* The terms of collaboration are adaptable, allowing for changes as trust and alignment grow.

**7. Expanded Access Over Time**  

* As the relationship matures, the user may gain broader rights to the IP, based on trust and mutual benefit.

**8. Joint Ventures**  

* Opportunities for co-creation and shared initiatives are encouraged as the collaboration evolves.

**9. Transition to Copyleft or Open Access**  

* The IP holder may choose to release their work into a donation-based or open-access model ("new Oasis") as their mission gains traction and financial sustainability.

**10. Values-Driven Collaboration**  

* The model prioritizes transparency, flexibility, and mutual benefit, ensuring the collaboration serves both the IP holder's mission and the user's goals.

These points can serve as a foundation for agreement between the IP holder and the user, fostering a transparent, ethical, and mutually beneficial relationship.
